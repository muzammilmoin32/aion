Aion Digital - Hero section mockups
===================================

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
Nothing to install. An internet connection is needed only for the fonts.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
Eight design options for the homepage hero (the first screen), stacked on one page.
Options 6-8 are the tilting glass-stage hero in three layouts (classic, centred, mirrored).
Use the Option 1-8 links under the header to jump between them.
Each option fills one screen. Click the journey chips above the phone to switch demos.

GOOD TO KNOW
- EN / عربي (top right) switches the whole page to Arabic, right to left.
- Business view / Technical view changes the captions on every demo.
- "Show what's behind it" (under the phone) opens the live trace of services called.
- Dark theme with a moving light stream; move the mouse over the phone to tilt it.
- Numbers, names and amounts with a dotted underline are sample values for this mockup.
- The phone videos are free stock sample clips (Pexels) standing in for the real
  screen recordings of each journey.
- Arabic text is a draft for review by a native Saudi reader.
